🎯 Executive Purpose
Systematic Crash Avoidance

Eliminate 50%+ catastrophic bear market drawdowns. Standard 60/40 or buy-and-hold suffers brutal drawdowns during secular crashes (2000-2002 Dot-com, 2008 GFC). Dual Momentum enforces rule-based risk offloading to safe-haven bonds before capital is destroyed.

📋 How to Use
Relative + Absolute Momentum Rules

1. Relative Momentum: Rank US Stocks vs International Stocks over trailing 12 months.
2. Absolute Momentum: If winner beats Cash/T-Bills (> 0% excess return), hold 100% in the winner.
3. Capital Defense: If winner trails Cash, rotate 100% to Aggregate Bonds / Treasuries.

🔍 What to Expect
Regime Shift Dynamics

• Secular Equity Bulls: Captures winning region (e.g., International in 1980s & 2000s; US Large Cap in 2010s).
• Severe Bear Crashes: Completely steps aside to bonds/cash in 2008 and 2000, slashing max drawdown from -50% to -25%.
• Turnover: Extremely low (≤ 1.4 trades/year), minimizing taxable friction.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Downside Protection & Sharpe Alpha

• Sharpe > 0.80: Significantly exceeds S&P 500 (~0.58) and 60/40 (~0.55).
• Max Drawdown Cut in Half: Prevents sequence-of-returns catastrophe for portfolios in accumulation or decumulation.
• Institutional Governance: Removes emotional panic and guesswork from asset allocation.
