🎯 Executive Purpose
Counter-Cyclical Discipline

Systematically fight human greed and fear. Long-term valuation metrics (Shiller CAPE, P/E) are the single strongest predictors of forward 10-year real returns. This model forces capital to take profits during speculative bubble peaks and buy when markets are discounted.

📋 How to Use
Dynamic Valuation Bounds

1. Equities Asset: Large Cap Blend (SPY).
2. Valuation Bands: Equity exposure scales between 80% (when market valuation is historically cheap) down to 30% (when valuation is in top 10% bubble territory).
3. Safe Haven Anchor: Allocate the remaining 20% to 70% to 10-Year Intermediate Treasuries.

🔍 What to Expect
Regime Shift Dynamics

• Late-Stage Melt-Ups: May trim exposure early during irrational bubble runs (e.g. 1998-1999 Dot-com), requiring emotional patience.
• Crash Aftermath: Sits comfortably in Treasuries as equities plunge, preserving dry powder to re-enter at generational bottoms (e.g. 1982, 2002, 2009).
• Turnover: Extremely low (≤ 0.5 trades/year), delivering institutional after-tax compounding.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Terminal Wealth & Risk-Adjusted Alpha

• Low Beta, High Alpha: Captures strong equity compounding while maintaining significantly lower equity beta across market cycles.
• Near-Zero Rebalancing Friction: Minimal transaction costs and negligible capital gains realization.
• Endowment Governance: Aligns with long-horizon fiduciaries and family office mandates.
