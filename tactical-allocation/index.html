🎯 Executive Purpose
Systematic Crash Protection

Eliminate catastrophic drawdowns. A 50% crash requires a 100% gain just to get back to even. Tactical allocation provides rule-based defense that shifts to cash/bonds before protracted bear markets destroy capital.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Rapid Workflow

1. Choose a quantitative timing model (Dual Momentum, 10M SMA, Momentum Rotation, Adaptive, Volatility Target, or Valuation).
2. Select safe-haven asset (Total Bond, Treasuries, or Cash).
3. Review historical risk metrics and live allocation regime.

🔍 What to Expect
Regime Shift Dynamics

• Secular Bull: Stays 100% invested in top momentum assets.
• Severe Crashes (2000 Dot-com, 2008 GFC): Rotates to cash/bonds, cutting max drawdowns in half (e.g., -25% vs -50%).
• Sideways Whipsaw: Choppy markets may trigger false breakout signals.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Downside Capture & Sharpe Ratio

• Max Drawdown Halved: Capital preservation guarantees you never panic-sell at market bottoms.
• Higher Sharpe Ratio (> 0.80): Superior capital efficiency per unit of total risk.
• Turnover (≤ 2x/year): Low transaction friction and manageable tax impact.
