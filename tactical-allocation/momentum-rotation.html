🎯 Executive Purpose
Cross-Asset Leadership Harvesting

Asset class returns are cyclical and non-stationary. Equities lead during economic expansions, commodities dominate during stagflation (1970s, 2022), and bonds thrive in deflation. Momentum rotation concentrates capital into what is working right now.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Relative Strength Workflow

1. Select Multi-Asset Pool: IVY 5 (US Stocks, Intl Stocks, Treasuries, REITs, Commodities).
2. Rank by Momentum: Compute trailing 6-month or 12-month returns across all assets.
3. Allocate to Top N: Hold the top 2 assets equally; if any top asset has negative return, shift its portion to Cash.

🔍 What to Expect
Regime Shift Dynamics

• Inflation Shocks (2022): When classic 60/40 portfolios plunged -18%, IVY 5 rotated into Commodities (+25%) and Cash, preserving wealth.
• Equity Bull Cycles: Concentrates in US Large Cap Growth and REITs.
• Turnover: Moderate (~4-6 rotations/year); best suited for tax-deferred accounts (401k, IRA) or tax-efficient ETF wrappers.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Diversification & Sortino Ratio

• True Regime Diversification: Replaces static asset weights with dynamic leadership selection.
• Sortino > 1.0: Generates superior risk-adjusted returns by eliminating downside volatility drag.
• Governance Simplicity: Clear, deterministic ranking eliminates emotional bias and forecasting errors.
