🎯 Executive Purpose
Overcoming the Parallel Shift Fallacy

Yield curves rarely shift in parallel. A bullet portfolio (100% in 10Y) and a barbell (50% in 2Y + 50% in 30Y) can have the exact same duration (~7.8Y), yet behave oppositely during yield curve steepeners or flatteners.

📋 How to Use
Curve Stress Workflow

1. Select Target Portfolio: Choose IEF, TLT, SHY, BND, or Barbell.
2. Pick Shift Scenario: Select Bull Steepener, Bear Flattener, or Butterfly Twist.
3. Inspect P&L Breakdown: Pinpoint which tenor contributed to profit or loss.

🔍 What to Expect
KRD Sum Identity

• Mathematical Law: $\sum_{k} KRD_k = D_{\text{eff}}$. Under a parallel shift, KRD gives the exact same result as modified duration.
• Non-Parallel Power: $\% \Delta P \approx -\sum KRD_k \cdot \Delta y_k$.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Institutional Regime Behavior

• Bull Steepener: Short rates fall faster $\rightarrow$ Barbells and short notes outperform.
• Butterfly Belly Selloff: 5Y/10Y intermediate yields spike $\rightarrow$ Bullets suffer while barbells preserve capital.
