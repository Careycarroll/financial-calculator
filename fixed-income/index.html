Quantify bond portfolio vulnerability to yield curve shifts. Calculate Macaulay Duration, Modified Duration, Convexity curvature, and DV01, or solve classical liability immunization barbells.

📋 How to Use
Pricing & Sensitivity Workflow

1. Input or Preset: Choose 10Y Benchmark, 30Y Long, or Corporate IG.
2. Solver Mode: Solve Price from YTM or solve exact YTM from market price.
3. Sensitivity: Inspect the 2nd-order Taylor series shock curve across -300 to +300 bps.

🔍 What to Expect
The Convexity Shield

• Duration Linearity Fallacy: Duration alone underestimates bond prices when yields move significantly.
• Positive Curvature: Bond prices rise faster when yields drop than they fall when yields rise.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Classical Immunization (Bodie Ch. 16)

• Duration Match: $D_A = D_L$ eliminates price sensitivity to parallel yield shifts.
• Convexity Shield: $C_A \ge C_L$ ensures asset values outgrow liability obligations under any yield shock.
