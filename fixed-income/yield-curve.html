🎯 Executive Purpose
De-Convoluting Yield Curves

Market Treasury quotes are par coupon yields, not true zero-coupon spot rates. Bootstrapping strips out intermediate coupon reinvestment noise to reveal the pure time-value of money.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Term Structure Workflow

1. Select Regime: Pick Current Treasury, Steep Normal (2018), or Inverted (2023).
2. Compare Curves: Contrast Par vs. Spot vs. 1Y Forward.
3. Price Forward Loans: Solve exact break-even rates for future borrowing windows.

🔍 What to Expect
Curve Order Theorem (Bodie Ch. 15)

• Upward Sloping: Forward Curve > Spot Curve > Par Curve.
• Inverted (Recession): Forward Curve < Spot Curve < Par Curve.
• Flat Curve: Forward = Spot = Par.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Liquidity Preference vs. Expectations

• Pure Expectations: Forward rates represent consensus market forecasts of future spot rates.
• Liquidity Preference: Forward rates include a positive term premium ($L_t$) compensating investors for interest rate volatility.
