🎯 Executive Purpose
Balance Sheet Defense

Ensure asset portfolios generate dependable cashflow to satisfy future contractual liabilities: pension distributions, debt maturities, deferred comp, and foundation grant commitments.

📋 How to Use
3-Step ALM Protocol

1. Starting Position: Enter current asset portfolio value and liability schedule.
2. Actuarial Parameters: Set discount rate and expected liability growth.
3. Inspect Cones: Review funded ratio trajectories across 10th to 90th percentiles.

🔍 What to Expect
Funded Ratio Dynamics

• Funded Ratio (Assets / PV Liabilities): >100% indicates surplus; <80% indicates underfunded danger.
• Rate Sensitivity: Higher discount rates reduce the present value of future liabilities.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Solvency Probability & Deficit Risk

• Deficit Probability: Likelihood of falling below 100% funded status.
• Cashflow Immunization: Duration-match fixed income to future cash obligations to eliminate market drawdown risk.
