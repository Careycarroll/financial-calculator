🎯 Executive Purpose
Solvency Engineering

Answer the existential question: Will our capital survive? Project portfolio longevity under thousands of randomized market paths to eliminate sequence-of-returns ruin.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Simulation Protocol

1. Configure Inputs: Set initial capital, horizon (e.g. 30Y), and annual withdrawal amount.
2. Set Simulation Model: Choose Historical Bootstrap or Parametric.
3. Run 1,000–10,000 Paths: Inspect survival curves and percentiles.

🔍 What to Expect
Sequence Risk Realities

• The Sequence Trap: Two portfolios with identical 8% average returns can have radically different outcomes; early crash years deplete capital before compounding can recover.
• Percentile Fanning: Cones widen over time; 10th percentile tests stress survival.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Probability of Solvency

• >90% Survival Rate: Institutional-grade confidence.
• 75%–90% Survival Rate: Vulnerable to sequence shocks; implement dynamic Guyton-Klinger withdrawal guardrails.
