🎯 Executive Purpose
Fee Justification & Unmasking Alpha

Active managers charge 100–150 bps claiming "alpha". Multivariate factor regression reveals whether that outperformance is true managerial genius or simply riding known systematic risk premia (Small Cap, Value, or Momentum).

📋 How to Use
3-Step Rapid Workflow

1. Select factor model (Fama-French 3-Factor, 5-Factor, or Carhart 4-Factor).
2. Choose focus fund.
3. Review regression loadings (β) and 36-month rolling style drift across bull/bear cycles.

🔍 What to Expect
Factor Loading Dynamics

• Value Funds (RZV/IWN): Expect positive β_HML (+0.5 to +0.9) and high R² (>90%).
• Style Drift: Notice how small-cap loadings drift larger during late-stage bull cycles as managers outgrow their universe.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Alpha Significance & R²

• Statistically Significant Alpha (p < 0.05): Keep the manager.
• Negative/Zero Alpha with high Factor Betas: Fire the manager; buy a 4 bps passive factor ETF instead.
• R² > 90%: The fund is an index hugger.
