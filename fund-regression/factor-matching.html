🎯 Executive Purpose
Fee Arbitrage & Synthetic Portfolios

Why pay 1.5%–2.0% active management fees when you can replicate a hedge fund's multi-factor exposure using 3–5 low-cost index ETFs at 0.05% expense ratio? Replicate target risk profiles with mathematical precision.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Rapid Workflow

1. Choose a target fund profile or enter custom factor betas (β_MKT, β_SMB, β_HML).
2. Check candidate replicating ETFs.
3. Run optimizer to solve optimal long-only weights (\sum w_i = 1, w_i ≥ 0).

🔍 What to Expect
Replication Dynamics

• Value & Size Targets: The optimizer blends Large Value (VTV) and Small Value (IJS).
• Factor Risk Parity: Equities receive lower capital weights while Bonds receive higher capital weights to equalize risk contribution.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Replication Fit R² & Tracking Error

• Replication Fit > 90%: Near-perfect factor replication; active returns are driven by systematic beta tilts rather than unique stock picking.
• RMSE < 0.10: Tight factor alignment across cycles.
