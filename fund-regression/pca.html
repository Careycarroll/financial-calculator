Decompose multi-asset return covariance into orthogonal statistical factors. Uncover the true dimensionality of portfolio risk, quantify broad market dominance, and identify independent risk drivers.

🎯 Executive Purpose
True Risk Dimensionality

Cutting through the "illusion of diversification". A portfolio with 20 equity funds may look diversified on paper, but if 1 component drives 85% of returns, it is effectively a single concentrated market bet.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Rapid Workflow

1. Select a fund universe or preset.
2. Choose Correlation Matrix (standard) or Covariance.
3. Inspect the Scree Plot elbow and review PC1/PC2 asset loadings.

🔍 What to Expect
Macro Factor Dynamics

• Equities: PC1 typically explains 75–90% of variance (the market tide).
• Multi-Asset: PC1 drops to 40–55%, with PC2 capturing duration/interest rate risk and PC3 capturing inflation/commodities.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Kaiser Rule (≥ 1.0) & Loadings

• Eigenvalue ≥ 1.0: Statistically significant independent risk driver.
• PC1 Dominance > 80%: High vulnerability to systemic shocks.
• Loadings: Positive on PC1 and opposite on PC2 indicates true hedging.
