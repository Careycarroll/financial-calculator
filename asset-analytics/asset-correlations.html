🎯 Executive Purpose
Uncover True Diversification

Eliminate cosmetic diversification. Holding 10 equity ETFs with 0.95+ correlation gives the illusion of safety while suffering 100% downside participation. Asset Correlations calculates real cross-asset diversification (bonds, cash, real estate, commodities) to insulate capital against systematic shocks.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Correlation Matrix Workflow

1. Configure Basket: Add 2 to 15 assets to the active basket.
2. Inspect Heatmap: Green/cyan cells (-0.20 to +0.20) indicate true shock absorbers; dark red (+0.80+) indicates redundant overlap.
3. Drill Down to Rolling Co-Movement: Click any table cell to graph its 36-month rolling correlation history.

🔍 What to Expect
Regime Dynamics & Flight-to-Quality

• Crisis Correlation Spikes: During liquidity crunches (2008, 2020), equity sectors converge toward 1.0.
• Flight to Safety: High-grade Treasuries and Cash typically decouple or turn inverse during equities selloffs.
• Inflation Regimes (2022): Both stocks and bonds plunged together, highlighting the necessity of commodities and cash hedges.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Average Correlation & Diversification Benefit

• Average Correlation < 0.40: High structural diversification and reduced portfolio volatility.
• Rolling Stability Range: Narrow rolling range indicates reliable diversification; wide swings indicate regime vulnerability.
• Capital Efficiency: Pairing uncorrelated assets boosts Sharpe ratio without lowering long-term expected returns.
