🎯 Executive Purpose
Multi-Factor Due Diligence

Eliminate expensive benchmark-huggers. Filter the fund universe by structural factor exposures, expense drag, and risk-adjusted performance to select best-in-class vehicles for allocation.

📋 How to Use
4-Step Screening Pipeline

1. Filter Universe: Select asset class, subcategory, and expense ceiling.
2. Track Record Hurdle: Require 3Y or 5Y history to eliminate short-term luck and survivorship bias.
3. Score & Rank: Rank by Sharpe, Sortino, or Alpha.
4. 1-Click Deep Dive: Click any fund row to open its full performance diagnostic.

🔍 What to Expect
Asymmetry & Market Capture

• Up/Down Capture Asymmetry: Target vehicles with Up Capture > 100% and Down Capture < 90% for long-term compound advantage.
• Jensen's Alpha (α): Positive alpha confirms genuine active management skill beyond market beta.
• Expense Drag: Every 50 bps in expense ratio compounds into tens of thousands in lost terminal wealth.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Percentile Ranks & Leaderboard

• Top Decile (Top 10%): Peer-group leaders delivering superior risk-adjusted efficiency.
• Sortino over Sharpe: In asymmetric strategies, Sortino reveals whether volatility was productive upside or destructive drawdowns.
• Max Drawdown Discipline: Lower drawdowns preserve capital and shorten recovery times.

🔵 Beta (β)
Market Sensitivity Multiplier

1.0 moves exactly with benchmark swings. >1.0 amplifies rallies and corrections; <1.0 provides defensive shock absorption.

🟢 Jensen's Alpha (α)
Pure Manager Edge

Excess annual return earned above CAPM market risk exposure. Any positive alpha proves institutional-grade skill.

⚖️ Capture Ratios
Asymmetric Compounding

Aim for Up-Market Capture > 100% and Down-Market Capture < 90% to grow faster in bull runs while losing less in downturns.

🏷️ Expense Ratio
Compound Fee Drag

Annual management fee deducted daily from NAV. Low fees (< 0.20%) protect tens of thousands in long-term wealth.
