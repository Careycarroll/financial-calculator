Market Sensitivity Multiplier

Annual management fee deducted daily from NAV. Low fees (< 0.20%) protect tens of thousands in long-term wealth.

💡 Quick Guide: How to Read Cointegration & Pairs Equilibrium Click to collapse / expand guide
▼
⚓ Cointegration vs Correlation
The Invisible Leash

Correlation measures temporary co-movement. Cointegration proves two prices share a permanent long-term mathematical equilibrium.

⚖️ Hedge Ratio (β)
Optimal Basket Multiplier

OLS regression slope determining the portfolio spread: Spread = Y − β×X. Isolates the stationary residual.

📉 ADF Test Statistic
Unit Root Rejection

Augmented Dickey-Fuller t-statistic must be more negative than the 95% critical value (-3.37) to confirm mean-reverting stationarity.

🎯 p-Value (< 0.05)
Pairs Trading & Rebalancing

Statistically significant p-values mean temporary price spreads reliably snap back, enabling low-risk mean-reversion rebalancing.
