Standard Institutional 3-Asset Allocation Model

This reference portfolio combines broad equities, high-dividend defensive equities, and total bond market exposure to demonstrate Markowitz diversification.

🏛️ Basket Constituents
• VOO (S&P 500 ETF): US core large-cap blend (~14.2% ann. volatility).
• SCHD (Schwab US Dividend Equity): Quality dividend value (~13.8% ann. volatility).
• BND (Total Bond Market): Fixed-income volatility dampener (~5.1% ann. volatility).
⏱️ Historical Horizon & Inputs
• Timeline: 2011-10 to Present (179 monthly returns bounded by SCHD inception).
• Risk-Free Rate ($R_f$): 3.00% (baseline cash / 3-month T-Bill yield).
• Covariance Estimator: Ledoit-Wolf Shrunk (institutional condition regularization).
• Investor Risk Aversion ($A$): 3.0 (balanced Bodie quadratic utility).
✨ The Diversification Free Lunch
Imperfect pairwise correlation between equities and bonds (ρ ≈ 0.05) causes portfolio variance
σp2 = w12σ12 + w22σ22 + 2w1w2Cov1,2
to bend the efficient frontier leftward, creating a combined portfolio with less risk than bonds alone.
🎯 Tangency Portfolio (Max Sharpe P*)
• Expected Output: Return ≈ 18.0% | Volatility ≈ 16.9% | Sharpe Ratio ≈ 0.890.
• Weights Mix: ~65% VOO, ~35% SCHD, 0% BND.
• Bodie Separation Property: Mathematically optimal risky basket offering maximum return per unit risk. All rational investors hold this identical risky portfolio and adjust risk solely by mixing with cash.
🛡️ Global Minimum Volatility (GMV)
• Expected Output: Volatility ≈ 4.72% | Return ≈ 4.12%.
• Weights Mix: ~85% BND, ~15% VOO, 0% SCHD.
• Key Takeaway: GMV achieves 4.72% volatility, which is lower than BND's individual 5.09% volatility! Adding 15% volatile stocks to pure bonds actually reduces overall portfolio risk.
📐 Capital Allocation Line (CAL) & C*
• Capital Allocation Line: The ray originating at $R_f = 3.0\%$ tangent to P*. Any point on this line dominates non-tangency portfolios.
• Complete Portfolio C*: With $A = 3.0$, optimal risky share y* ≈ 100%, cash 1-y* ≈ 0%. Bodie utility is maximized: $U^* = E(r_c) - 0.5 \cdot A \cdot \sigma_c^2 \approx 13.7\%$.
🎨 Asset Allocation Transition Ribbon
• Smooth Asset Substitution: Inspect the stacked area chart below the frontier. As target volatility scales up from GMV (4.7%) to Max Return (18.0%), BND steadily drops from 85% to 0%, while high-beta equity (VOO) expands to drive terminal returns.
Input Parameter Adjustment Economic Intuition & What to Expect in the Outputs
Investor Risk Aversion (A) Increase (A = 3.0 → 6.0+) Complete portfolio C* slides down the CAL toward Cash ($R_f$). Optimal risky share $y^*$ drops from 100% down to ~50%, holding 50% cash. Sharpe ratio remains identical, but portfolio volatility is halved to preserve capital against drawdowns.
Investor Risk Aversion (A) Decrease (A = 3.0 → 1.5) Complete portfolio C* slides up the CAL past 100% into leverage ($y^* > 100\%$). The investor borrows at the borrowing rate to buy $>100\%$ of the Tangency portfolio, amplifying expected return without compromising diversification.
Covariance Estimator Sample → Ledoit-Wolf Sample covariance suffers from "error maximization" (allocating extreme 100% or 0% bets to noisy historical correlations). Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage dampens corner portfolios, reduces condition number κ by ~67%, and yields robust out-of-sample stability.
Risk-Free Rate (Rf) Increase (3.0% → 5.0%) Flattens the slope of the Capital Allocation Line. Modest-return defensive assets fail the higher hurdle rate; the Tangency portfolio P* shifts rightward toward higher-growth equity assets.
Asset Weight Bounds Set Max Weight ≤ 35% Frontier shifts slightly inward (slight loss in theoretical unconstrained efficiency), but eliminates single-asset blow-up risk and enforces institutional portfolio diversification.
