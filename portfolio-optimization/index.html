🎯 Executive Purpose
Optimal Risk-Return Efficiency

Identify the mathematical boundary of portfolios offering maximum expected return for a given level of risk. Eliminate sub-optimal allocations that take uncompensated volatility.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Frontier Setup

1. Select Basket: Add assets across equities, bonds, real estate, and cash.
2. Set Risk-Free Rate: Configure baseline cash yield.
3. Inspect Tangency: Locate the Maximum Sharpe portfolio on the Capital Allocation Line.

🔍 What to Expect
The Free Lunch of Diversification

• Frontier Curvature: Imperfect correlations bend the frontier leftward, reducing volatility without sacrificing return.
• Global Minimum Volatility (GMV): The absolute lowest risk portfolio possible.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Tangency vs Cash Lending

• Tangency Portfolio: Mathematically superior risk-adjusted asset mix.
• Capital Allocation Line (CAL): Blend Tangency portfolio with cash to reach exact target risk without suboptimal stock picking.
