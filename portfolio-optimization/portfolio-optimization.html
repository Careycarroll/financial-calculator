🎯 Executive Purpose
Targeted Risk Budgeting

Move beyond arbitrary 60/40 splits. Solve for optimal asset allocations aligned with specific mandates: Maximize Sharpe, Minimize Volatility (GMV), Risk Parity (ERC), or Tail-Risk CVaR.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Objective Setup

1. Select Mandate: Choose Sharpe, GMV, Risk Parity, or CVaR.
2. Set Constraints: Cap asset bounds (e.g. 5% min, 35% max) to avoid single-asset corner bets.
3. Compare Outcomes: Benchmark against Equal-Weighted and 60/40.

🔍 What to Expect
Risk Parity vs Market Cap

• The 60/40 Fallacy: A 60/40 portfolio derives ~90% of its risk from equities.
• Equal Risk Contribution (ERC): Allocates more weight to low-volatility assets so every asset contributes equally to risk.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Tail-Risk CVaR vs Sharpe

• Max Sharpe: Maximizes mean efficiency during standard market regimes.
• Min CVaR (Expected Shortfall): Minimizes losses in the worst 5% crash months during systemic liquidity crises.
