Evaluate dynamic walk-forward portfolio optimization strategies out-of-sample. 🎯 Executive Purpose
Truth in Optimization

Eliminate in-sample backtest illusions. Static backtests fit noise from past data; walk-forward optimization rigorously evaluates whether dynamic re-optimization actually works in unseen future market regimes.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Walk-Forward Protocol

1. Select Strategy: Max Sharpe, Minimum Volatility, or Risk Parity.
2. Set Windows: Choose lookback period (e.g. 36M) and rebalance frequency (Annual/Quarterly).
3. Run Simulation: Review out-of-sample compound trajectory.

🔍 What to Expect
Turnover & Weight Churn

• Turnover Drag: High parameter sensitivity triggers aggressive reallocations, incurring friction.
• Risk Parity Stability: Produces smoother out-of-sample allocations with lower turnover than unconstrained Max Sharpe.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Live Edge vs Overfitting

• Out-of-Sample Sharpe: If out-of-sample Sharpe drops significantly below in-sample Sharpe, the model is overfit.
• Benchmark Tracking: Ensures active dynamic optimization generates true excess return over buy-and-hold.
