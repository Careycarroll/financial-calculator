🎯 Executive Purpose
Overcoming Markowitz Instability

Classic Markowitz is notoriously an "error-maximizer" that produces wild, uninvestable 0% and 100% weights. Black-Litterman anchors on market equilibrium (CAPM prior) and gently blends in active views.

📋 How to Use
3-Step Bayesian Workflow

1. Equilibrium Benchmark: Start with reverse-optimized market cap weights.
2. Express Views: Add absolute (Asset X = +8%) or relative (Asset X beats Asset Y by 3%) views.
3. Calibrate Confidence: Set slider from 10% to 100%.

🔍 What to Expect
Confidence Shrinkage Dynamics

• High Confidence (>80%): Generates decisive active tilts toward your favored assets.
• Low Confidence (<30%): Bayesian math automatically shrinks weights back toward the market cap prior.

💡 Executive Output Decoder
Institutional Governance & Tilts

• Implied Equilibrium Return (Π): What the market expects assets to earn.
• Blended Posterior Return: Sensible, balanced return forecasts that produce realistic portfolio weights.
