ℹ️ How the Safety Switch Works

10-Month Trend Following Defense
At the end of each month, the model checks whether the asset price is above or below its 10-month moving average. If the asset is in an established downtrend (below SMA), its target allocation is automatically shifted into your chosen Defensive Reserve Asset (Cash/Treasuries). When the asset crosses back above its 10-month average, full exposure is restored.
